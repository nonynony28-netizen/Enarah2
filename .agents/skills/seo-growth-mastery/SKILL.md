Always enforce valid JSON-LD schemas in `index.html` and page components:
- **`LightingStore` / `LocalBusiness`:**
  - `name`: "الإنارة الحديثة"
  - `addressLocality`: "بنغازي", `addressCountry`: "LY"

## 🚀 3. Sitemaps & Technical SEO (الخرائط والأرشفة الفورية)

- Keep `public/sitemap.xml` updated with all active routes with priority `0.70` to `1.00`.
- Maintain `public/robots.txt` allowing all legitimate crawlers and pointing to `https://enarahmodern.com/sitemap.xml`.
- Maintain Google Search Console verification via both `<meta name="google-site-verification">` and root HTML file.
- Enforce self-referential `<link rel="canonical" href="https://enarahmodern.com/">` to avoid duplicate content penalties.
